Storage & privacy

Entries are saved in this browser on this device, not sent to a server. No account or cloud sync is included. Clearing website data, changing the website address, private browsing, or losing your device may remove access to your entries. Back up regularly. This app has no passcode or added encryption layer. Anyone with access to this browser may see your entries. This is a personal journal, not a medical or emergency service.
